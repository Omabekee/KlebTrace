# Data

All files here are generated by `scripts/query_portal.py` from the EMBL-EBI AMR Portal Parquet release, except `samples.tsv`, which `scripts/finalise_study_set.py` then updates (see below), and `genomes_manifest.tsv`.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `portal_isolates.tsv` | Every *K. pneumoniae* isolate with an assembly and a meropenem result: phenotype, country, year, region and any carbapenem-class genes reported by the portal |
| `portal_summary.tsv` | Isolate counts by world region, phenotype and carbapenemase status |
| `samples.tsv` | The seven study isolates and the group each was selected for |
| `genomes_manifest.tsv` | Size, sequence count, N50, GC, MD5 and check result for each downloaded genome, from `scripts/download_genomes.py` |
| `portal_calls.tsv` | Every portal genotype call for the seven study assemblies (rewritten by `finalise_study_set.py`), used for comparison with KlebTrace's own annotation. The portal lists a call once per antibiotic it affects (`split_subclass`), so some rows repeat |

## Selection rules

- **Phenotype.** An isolate is resistant if any meropenem test was resistant, and susceptible only if every test was susceptible. Intermediate results are excluded.
- **Carbapenemase status.** Based on AMRFinderPlus calls (`amr_element_symbol`, not `gene_symbol`, which is the assembly's own label and can be generic, wrong or empty) in the portal with a subclass containing "CARBAPENEM" and an element subtype other than POINT, so acquired carbapenemase genes (such as blaKPC and blaNDM) count, while porin point mutations (such as ompC or ompK36 changes) are listed separately in `carbapenem_point_mutations`.
- **Assemblies.** Only isolates with a GenBank or RefSeq assembly accession (GCA_ or GCF_) are eligible, so every genome can be downloaded as a complete assembly and matched to Ensembl.
- **Study set from the portal's calls (six genomes).**
  - Three resistant isolates, one per carbapenemase family (NDM, KPC, OXA-48-like), each with only that one carbapenemase gene.
  - Two resistant isolates with no carbapenemase: one with a carbapenem porin point mutation, preferring Africa, and one with no such mutation.
  - One susceptible control with no carbapenemase.
- **Strict resistance (`strict_resistant`), required for the two isolates with no carbapenemase.** The MIC must exceed 8 mg/L and the isolate must be resistant under both `Updated_phenotype_CLSI` and `Updated_phenotype_EUCAST`. Only mg/L measurements count, so disk-zone values in mm are ignored. `measurement_sign` is respected: `>8` and `>=16` pass, while `<=16` and `>=8` do not. Every mg/L test for an isolate must pass.
- **Re-check of the no-carbapenemase isolates, and the seventh genome.** The assemblies of the 37 strict-resistant, GCA_ isolates the portal reports without a carbapenemase were re-screened at the nucleotide level with a split-gene check (`scripts/screen_carbapenemases.py`, results in `results/portal_negative_screen.tsv`); the screen negatives were then run through Kleborate, and the study genomes through AMRFinderPlus in Galaxy.
  - *References:* the 568 alleles in Kleborate's AMR database v3.3 of class Bla_Carb described as carbapenem-hydrolysing, excluding CTX-M (CTX-M-33 is labelled carbapenem-hydrolysing and would otherwise match CTX-M-15; OXA-233 is also dropped by the description filter).
  - *Alignment:* minimap2 2.31 (`-c -k 11 -w 3 -N 50 --secondary=yes`), keeping alignments of at least 50 bp and 95% identity.
  - *Calls per allele:* the reference positions covered by all its alignments are combined. **Intact**: one alignment covers at least 95% of the allele. **Split**: the alignments together cover at least 95%, and every piece runs into a contig end (within 10 bp), as expected when the assembly breaks inside the gene. **Interrupted**: the same coverage, but a piece does not reach a contig end. **Partial**: less than 95%. The best allele per genome is chosen by call, then matching bases, then fewest pieces.
  - *Result:* 2 split (KPC-2 in GCA_001182445.1, OXA-232 in GCA_019928365.1), 3 partial, 32 none. For GCA_019928365.1, 291 Illumina reads from SRR26412251 cross the join between the two contig ends. No reads were found for GCA_001182445.1.
  - `scripts/finalise_study_set.py` relabels the two split genomes `portal_negative_carbapenemase_on_reannotation` and adds one `resistant_no_carbapenemase_confirmed` genome: screen call none and no carbapenemase in Kleborate (`results/kleborate_negatives/`), ranked by known region, then fewest sequences, then most recent. This picked GCA_000567225.2.
- **Ranking within each group.** In Ensembl first, then known region, then a region not yet used, then regions named with `--prefer-regions`, then most recent. The study set was selected with `--ensembl-list species_EnsemblBacteria.txt --prefer-regions Africa`, then finalised with `python scripts/finalise_study_set.py --kleborate results/kleborate_negatives/general__species_output.txt`.

## Provenance

- AMR Portal release: `2026-07`
- Date queried: `2026-11-02`
- Ensembl species list: `species_EnsemblBacteria.txt`, built from Ensembl Bacteria release 63 core databases, not stored in this repository. Download URL: https://ftp.ensemblgenomes.ebi.ac.uk/pub/bacteria/current/species_EnsemblBacteria.txt
- Genome FASTA files are downloaded from ENA by assembly accession with `scripts/download_genomes.py` and are not stored in this repository. Headers are shortened from `ENA|X|X.1 description` to `X.1 description`.
